Dualo Privacy Support

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email dualo@conov.io.

Do I need an OpenAI account?

No. Dualo can download a model that runs entirely on your device, with no account and no internet connection once it's downloaded. An OpenAI API key is optional and adds OpenAI's cloud models.

Who pays for OpenAI models?

Messages to OpenAI models use your own API key, so OpenAI bills your OpenAI account for them. You can get a key at platform.openai.com. On-device models are free to use.

Where are my chats stored?

Only on your device. A chat goes to OpenAI only when it uses an OpenAI model, after you've allowed it. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete a chat?

Open your chats from the home screen, then swipe left on a chat and tap Delete.

How do I stop sending chats to OpenAI?

In Settings, switch off Send Chats to OpenAI. Dualo will ask again before sending anything else. Chats on on-device models are never sent anywhere.

The on-device model is slow or won't load

On-device models need a recent iPhone with enough free memory. Closing other apps can help. If a download fails, check that you have enough free storage and try again from Settings, under Local Models.
